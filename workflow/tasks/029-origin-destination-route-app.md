## Link existing actual ML to route, no fake extrapolation
- Route must render immediately and remain usable before/without research /ready. Separate route service and forecast service states.
- For actual route bbox, fetch all pages of actual qualified RoadAware segments (500/page, sequential with progress, max geometry bounds/span). DO NOT treat first page as all route coverage. - Match accepted road segment geometry to route conservatively using real geometry proximity/overlap. Define and document a client comparison rule (e.g. sample segment along length ~100m, >=50% of sampled segment length within50m of route) to avoid assigning an intersecting cross street's forecast to the route. A single nearest midpoint or bbox intersection is insufficient. Any spatial match is approximate candidate linkage, never validated navigation-grade coverage. Label near-route forecast matches honestly. Avoid huge main-thread nested loops: prefilter by segment/route bounding boxes or simple spatial index, yield/progress on larger routes.

Do not use province extrapolation, fake values, universal risk, or sum counts into route probability. Circles at actual matched geometry along the route, not random/pattern dots. Show matched count and limitations, don't claim unsupported proportion exactly without validated overlap length.
- Markers are screen-space symbols for ~2km model segments, NOT a proven hazard radius or exact crash locations. Clicking circle synchronizes a useful Thai result/history panel from that segment and actual model response. Reuse human-readable prediction/history cards, with research scenario limitations.
